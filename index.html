How to train well
  1. Prop the phone up so your mouth is centred and evenly lit.
  2. Record 8–15 takes per word. Start and end each take with your lips closed and relaxed.
  3. Record _noise takes: smiling, yawning, swallowing, talking to someone. Anything closest to a word starting with “_” is ignored.
  4. Use Check before trusting Decode.
